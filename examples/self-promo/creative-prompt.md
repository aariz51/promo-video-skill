# PRODUCT-SPECIFIC PROMO PROMPT

Source of creative structure: MASTER CREATIVE FRAMEWORK (framework/master-creative-framework.md)
Product: promo-video
Target runtime: 85 s · Master: 1920×1080 16:9 · Adaptations: 9:16, 1:1

## CORE MESSAGE

Your repository already holds the story; promo-video directs it into a film and checks
its own work. Three carrying ideas: **UNDERSTAND · DIRECT · VERIFY**.

## CREATIVE DNA

- §4 arc: mystery before name, name before structure, proof before breadth → the film opens
  on fragments of a repository (file names, commands) orbiting a point of light before the
  name appears; the structure (understand · direct · verify) comes before any demonstration.
- §5 pacing: 5–8 s beats, holds after reveals, one acceleration, still ending → calm hook,
  a long hero demonstration, a faster run through the pipeline and the refusal, then a
  near-silent lockup.
- §6 show, don't tell: input → process → result → no adjectives on screen. Real `promo`
  commands and their real output, the real brief with real claims, the real error the
  compiler raises, stills of the real film.
- §7–9 visual, motion, typography: graphite ground, one blue accent, Inter for statements,
  JetBrains Mono for machine output; type only where the words are the subject.
- §10–11 sound and narration: no background music; keystrokes, ticks, pops and one chime
  tied to what happens; seven short lines of narration, many beats silent.
- §12 factual accuracy: only claims C1…C14 from the brief; no users, speed or quality claims.

## PACING PLAN

Hook 8 s (freeze at 4.6 s) · title 5 s · shape 7 s · proof 10 s · hero 13 s + 12 s ·
unexpected capability 9 s · two paths 7 s · formats 7 s · trust 7 s · lockup 8 s = 93 s.
Holds: 1.5 s after the title, after the brief's last section, after the refusal. The
acceleration is the pipeline flow into the refusal; the lockup is the still ending.

## MOTION & VISUAL SYSTEM

Graphite ground throughout, accent #6E9BFF. Point of light opens and closes the film.
Push transitions into demonstrations, light transitions at the two act breaks (title,
trust), a wipe into the refusal. Terminal and document scenes carry the product; they
settle out of a slight tilt and drift. One focal object per frame.

## SOUND DESIGN

No background music. Near-silence under the hook until the fragments accelerate; a riser
into the freeze and an impact on it. Keystrokes while commands type, ticks as output
arrives, pops as steps land, one chime when the pipeline reaches the film. Silence before
the lockup line.

## NARRATION

Voice direction: calm, precise, unhurried; a senior engineer describing something they
trust. Lines exactly as in the scene plan; seven lines in 93 seconds.

## ORIGINALITY

Built from the master framework and this repository's own material only. No third-party
footage, logos or wording. Every terminal line is real output from this repository.
