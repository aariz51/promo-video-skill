## 5. MOTION LANGUAGE

- Transitions are built from the product's own gestures: select, scale, drag, draw a line. The camera never cuts when a gesture can carry it.
- Kinetic typography is minimal: small lines type in; only one word ever becomes large, and it becomes a doorway (fly through a letter's counter, 0:04).
- Depth transitions: the camera moves *into* things (a letter, a text field, a page) rather than across them.
- Data transformation: the growth chart is a camera-panned world several screens wide, with the brand mark riding the head of the line (0:14–0:21); the line is drawn only up to the camera.
- UI animation: collaborator cursors with avatars glide in, hover, select; a comment bubble types (0:12).
- Parallax: floating 3D props at different depths around the prompt card (0:06–0:09).
- Principle: every motion is something a user of the product could do; there are no decorative effects.
