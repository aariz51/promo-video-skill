# PRODUCT-SPECIFIC PROMO PROMPT

Source of creative structure: YOUTUBE INSPIRATIONAL PROMPT (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sMVY15d7BA)
Product: promo-video
Target runtime: 67 s (source 26.7 s, time scale ×2.5) · Master: 1920×1080 16:9 · Adaptations: 9:16, 1:1

## CORE MESSAGE

Give it your repository and a film you admire; it borrows the film's grammar and keeps
your product's story. Three carrying ideas: **WATCH · BORROW · KEEP YOUR STORY**.

## CREATIVE DNA

- R1 — Open at the smallest scale → one short monospace line typed into an empty warm canvas.
- R2 — The product's own gesture is the first edit → cursors tagged "you" and "agent" select one word, as in an editor.
- R3 — The brand is a place the camera arrives in → we fly through the counter of a letter into the accent ground, where the name lands.
- R4 — Continuous camera travel; hard cuts only on turns → push transitions between demonstrations; hard cuts only into "not a template" and into the wall of output.
- R5 — Input before output, any input welcome → the real `promo init` / `promo watch` commands, the inspiration marked optional.
- R6 — Output in its real environment → the real inspiration-analysis.md, sections writing in.
- R7 — The problem as a diagram, not a claim → copied versus borrowed, side by side. (The source's growth chart is not reused: this product has no growth data.)
- R8 — The mark rides the data → dropped: there is no time-series to ride. Its intent (the brand moving with the process) carries into the pipeline flow, where the pulse travels to the film.
- R9 — Sentences under eight words, built as spoken → every on-screen line is short and builds word by word.
- R10 — Range once, as a dense wall → a grid of real frames from this product's other film.
- R11 — End on proof, then the mark → one finished frame pushed into, then the name alone.
- R12 — One saturated colour for everything interactive, neutral canvas → paper preset, blue accent.
- R13 — Depth through blur and scale → push transitions with blur; no 3D gloss.
- R14 — Every motion is a user gesture → typing, selecting, commands running, sections writing in.

## PACING PLAN

Source proportions scaled to 67 s: hook with brand arrival 9 s (brand lands at 6 s, held 3 s) ·
input 9 s · output 10 s · hard cut · problem 9 s · difference 10 s · hard cut · range 7 s ·
proof 6 s · lockup 7 s. The two hard cuts are the only cuts, as in the source. Holds on the
brand arrival and on the final name; acceleration through range into proof.

## MOTION & VISUAL SYSTEM

Paper ground (warm off-white) for the whole film, one blue accent for selection, cursors,
the flow pulse and the brand ground. Monospace for the typed hook and commands; Inter for
statements. Camera: pushes with blur between demonstrations, a zoom into the proof frame,
two hard cuts. No device chrome except the terminal window and the product's own frames.

## SOUND DESIGN

No background music, unlike the source's music bed: keystrokes while text types, a click on
the selection, a whoosh through the letter, an impact on the brand ground, ticks as the
analysis writes, pops as cards land, one chime when the pipeline reaches the film, silence
under the wall of frames, a soft resolve under the name.

## NARRATION

Voice direction: calm, warm, conversational, second person; short sentences like the
source's density (about half the runtime spoken). Lines exactly as in the scene plan.

## ORIGINALITY

Kept from the source as structure: the tiny opening line, selection as the first edit, the
fly-through into the brand ground, input → output order, the problem as a diagram, one wall
of range, ending on proof then the name, two hard cuts at the turns, sentence length.
Replaced: every word, every visual and every claim; the growth chart (no data here) became a
copied-versus-borrowed comparison; the music bed became gesture-tied effects. No footage,
logo, wording, product UI or narration from the source appears.
