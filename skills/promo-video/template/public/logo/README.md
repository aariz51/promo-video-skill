# logo

Put your app logo here as `app-logo.png` (this is what `LOGO` in `src/theme.ts` points to).

A square logo that includes the wordmark works best for the final logo-lockup scene
(`src/scenes/S9_Logo.tsx`), where it's shown as a rounded app-icon tile with a soft
shadow and a heartbeat pulse. If your logo is mark-only, add the wordmark as text in
that scene instead.
